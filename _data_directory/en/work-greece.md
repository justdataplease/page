|---------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| series_name_en                              | What the series measures, e.g. “Unemployment (monthly) — All ages · Total”. |
| sex, age                                    | Total, men or women, and the age group, e.g. under 25. |
| nace_r2, nace_r2_1                          | The economic sector, e.g. manufacturing or accommodation and food. |
| unit_label_en                               | % of the labour force, thousand people, € or purchasing power standards. |
| geo, geo_name_en, geo_level                 | Greece, a region, the EU27, the euro area or an EU country, and its level. |
| is_greece                                   | TRUE for Greece and its regions. |
| period_date, year, quarter, semester, month | The first day of the period and its parts. |
| value, value_prev_year                      | The value as the source publishes it, and the same period a year earlier. |
| is_latest                                   | TRUE on the latest value of each series. |
