| **Column**                        | **Description** |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------|
| geo_level, geo_name_gr, region_gr | The country, one of the 13 regions or a municipality, with its region. |
| month                             | The month of the listings; the last one is the previous full month. |
| listing_kind                      | sale or rent. |
| price_sqm                         | The asking price in € per m² (for rent, per month). |
| index_2022, change_yoy            | The 2022 average = 100, and the change from the same month a year earlier. |
| median_price, median_sqm          | The typical home: median price and size of the last 12 months' listings. |
| feature_gr, value_gr              | Price per m² by size, construction year and type of home. |
| gross_yield, years_of_rent        | A year's rent over the sale price, and how many years of rent "buy" the home. |
| is_reliable                       | Enough listings for the area's price to stand on its own. |

**NOTE** - Source: our own collection of homes listed for sale and for rent. These are asking prices, not sale prices, and deals usually close lower; for transaction prices see the Bank of Greece index in the Housing topic. The rental yield is gross: before tax, costs and empty months.
