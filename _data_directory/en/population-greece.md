| **Column**                             | **Description** |
|----------------------------------------|-----------------|
| year                                   | Population on 1 January, migration during the year. |
| geo_code, geo_name, geo_level          | The country or the region. |
| sex, age_group                         | Men or women, in five-year age groups from 0-4 to 85+. |
| population_count                       | Residents of the area, sex and age group. |
| share_age_0_14, share_age_65_plus      | Children and older people as a share of the population. |
| old_age_dependency_per_100             | Residents aged 65+ per 100 residents aged 15–64. |
| population_change_yoy                  | The difference from the previous year. |
| measure, migrant_count                 | How many people arrived and how many left, by sex and age. |
| registered, voted, resident_population | Registered voters, ballots cast and residents of each region at each election. |

**NOTE** - Sources: ELSTAT and, for voters, the Ministry of Interior. The resident population is not the electoral roll, and the historical milestones refer to a different territory each time. The data are credited to ELSTAT as the source; we rearranged them, and the sources are not responsible for the changes.
