|---|---|
| month, area, neighbourhood | The snapshot month, the area and the neighbourhood or municipality. |
| room_type, listings | Entire homes, rooms and how many there are. |
| price | The median price per night of an entire home. |
| occupancy | How many nights of the next month are booked or blocked. |
| host size, licence | How many listings the host has and whether a licence number is shown. |
| airbnb_per_rent_listing | Homes on Airbnb for every home offered for long-term rent, per municipality and neighbourhood. |
