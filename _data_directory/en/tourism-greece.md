description_detailed: "Nights spent at tourist accommodation (hotels, rented rooms and other short-stay accommodation, campsites) every month from 1994 to July 2026, separately for residents of the country and of abroad, for Greece, the EU27, the euro area and the EU countries, and arrivals at that accommodation for Greece and the EU27. Alongside them are the country's travel receipts and payments every month since 2002 (Bank of Greece), every quarter and every year for all EU countries (Eurostat), and, by region and regional unit, the nights, establishments and bed places of each year (1994–2025). From January to July 2026 Greece recorded 84.6 million nights, 1.6% more than in the same months of 2025 (EU27: +1.9%), with 21.2 million arrivals (+1.0%) and travel receipts of €13,330 million (+10.4%). In 2025 nights reached 156.3 million, fifth in the EU after Italy, Spain, France and Germany and 8.9% above 2019; 27% were spent in the South Aegean."
description_preprocess: "The Eurostat tables are read in full from its API at every update, every month and year, because recent values are often estimates and get revised. The Bank of Greece's monthly balance-of-payments file is downloaded from its page at every update, archived unchanged (with its SHA-256 fingerprint) and read in full. Eurostat values are first kept exactly as they arrived, with all the source codes, and the final table is checked to have exactly the same rows per table. Cross-check: the Bank of Greece's monthly receipts, summed per quarter, match Eurostat's quarterly figures in all 74 quarters of 2008–2026, within €0.5 million; a fixed check point is the receipts of January 2002, €170.8 million. The Greek names of the regions and series are our own. Year-to-date figures are sums of the months of one series and one area, from January to the latest available month, against the same months of the previous year; in EU rankings a country counts only if it has all 12 months of the year. Nights, arrivals and receipts are never added to each other, nor the country to its regions; regional units are parts of regions, so they never go into the same sum. Receipts are in current prices, not adjusted for inflation. Gaps: arrivals of non-residents at the border, which the Bank of Greece publishes quarterly by country in another file, are not included; arrivals at accommodation are given only for Greece and the EU27; the latest months are often flagged e (estimated)."

**NOTE** - Sources: Eurostat, tour_occ_nim (nights, monthly), tour_occ_arm (arrivals, monthly), tour_occ_nin2 (nights by region), tour_cap_nuts2 (establishments and bed places by region), bop_c6_q and bop_its6_det (travel receipts, quarterly and annual); Bank of Greece, bog_bop_monthly (monthly balance of payments: travel receipts and payments). ELSTAT collects the accommodation data for Greece, and we read them as Eurostat distributes them. Nights cover only the surveyed accommodation; short-term rentals through platforms are in the Airbnb topic. Travel receipts include everything visitors from abroad spend in the country, not only on accommodation. The stats_eu_rank table gives Greece's position in the EU for each series and period. Eurostat data may be reused freely with acknowledgement of the source; figures that come from ELSTAT are credited to ELSTAT. We translated and rearranged the Eurostat, ELSTAT and Bank of Greece data, and they are not responsible for the changes.
