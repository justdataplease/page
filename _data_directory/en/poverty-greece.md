| **Column**                  | **Description** |
|-----------------------------|-----------------|
| series_name_en              | What the series measures, e.g. “At risk of poverty or social exclusion — All ages”. |
| age                         | All ages, under 18, or 65 and over. |
| unit_label_en               | Share of the population, euro, purchasing power standards, Gini or the S80/S20 ratio. |
| geo, geo_name_en, geo_level | Greece, a region, the EU27, the euro area or an EU country, and its level. |
| is_greece                   | TRUE for Greece and its regions. |
| period_date, year           | The survey year and its first day. |
| value, value_prev_year      | The value as the source publishes it, and the same period a year earlier. |
| status_flag                 | p provisional, e estimated, b break in series. |
| is_latest                   | TRUE on the latest value of each series. |

**NOTE** - Source: Eurostat, EU-SILC survey, carried out in Greece by ELSTAT. Each year's survey measures the previous year's income, so the 2025 survey shows 2024 incomes. The data may be reused with Eurostat and ELSTAT credited as sources; we translated and rearranged them, and neither is responsible for the changes.
