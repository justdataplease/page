We encourage the use of our data and visualizations, but proper attribution is required. If you use any data, graphs, or insights from DataForGreece, whether embedded, modified, or as a screenshot, you must include a credit with a link back to the source.

How to Credit DataForGreece

Proper Attribution Format

If you are embedding or referencing a specific dataset or visual, include a direct link to that dataset. For example, if you decide to use this visual, you should include one of the following.

Source: DataForGreece

Data provided by DataForGreece

Example Usage

If you embed or take a screenshot of a graph, the credit should appear clearly in the publication. Example:

Example Reference:

The latest data from DataForGreece shows an increase in olive oil prices during July 2023.

Example Graph:

Brand Guidelines

To ensure proper representation of DataForGreece, follow these brand guidelines:

  • Use our official logo without modifications.
  • Maintain clear readability of the brand name.
  • Ensure proper contrast and background for visibility.

Download Brand Assets

Additional Notes

  • Commercial usage of our data requires permission. Contact us at info@dataforgreece.com.
  • Data and visualizations cannot be altered in a misleading way.
  • Proper attribution enhances transparency and supports open data initiatives.
