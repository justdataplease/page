{%- comment -%} Prints a title with an en dash in its year ranges ("2000-2023" -> "2000–2023"), for display only: the article files are mirrored from the original site and keep their hyphens. Only a digit-hyphen-digit joint is touched, so "COVID-19" stays as it is. Usage: {% include years.html text=doc.title %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _yr = include.text | append: "" -%} {%- if _yr contains "-" -%} {%- for _d in (0..9) -%} {%- assign _y1 = _d | append: "-1" -%}{%- assign _y1n = _d | append: "–1" -%} {%- assign _y2 = _d | append: "-2" -%}{%- assign _y2n = _d | append: "–2" -%} {%- assign _yr = _yr | replace: _y1, _y1n | replace: _y2, _y2n -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endif -%} {{- _yr -}}
