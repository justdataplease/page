We put three things side by side: the entire homes rented out on Airbnb today, the dwellings counted by the **2021 census**, and the guest nights that **Eurostat** records through the short-term rental platforms. The picture changes a lot from place to place.

- **Mykonos and Santorini:** about 3 homes on Airbnb for every 10 dwellings on the island. In Athens, 3 in 100.
- **The smaller Cyclades:** on Milos, Tinos, Sifnos and Naxos there are three times as many homes on Airbnb, or more, as the census found empty and for rent.
- **Platforms:** nights booked through Airbnb, Booking and the other platforms more than doubled since 2019, and in 2025 they were about 1 in 4 paid nights in the country. In central Athens, almost 1 in 2.

## On the islands, Airbnb is a large share of the homes

Mykonos now has **322 entire homes on Airbnb per 1,000 dwellings**, Santorini 280, Milos 228 and Paros 213. Chania has 89 and Rhodes 79. The big cities are far lower: 32 in Athens, 20 in Thessaloniki and Heraklion.

The municipality of Athens has the most homes on Airbnb in absolute terms (14,031), but relative to the size of the city Airbnb is much thinner there than on the islands.

## More homes on Airbnb than were standing empty for rent

The 2021 census also recorded how many homes were **empty and for rent**. On Milos, Tinos and Sifnos there are now more than 3 homes on Airbnb for each of them, on Naxos 3 and on Paros 2.2. On Mykonos and Santorini the ratio is 1.7 and 1.4.

{% include article-chart.html id="airbnb-census-per-vacant-for-rent" %}

On these islands, year-round renting was a small part of the market to begin with. On Naxos only 21% of the occupied homes had a tenant in 2021, and in Apokoronas near Chania 15%, against 42% in Athens. Holiday and secondary homes, on the other hand, were about half of all homes on Paros and Naxos. Many of the Airbnb homes probably come from that stock rather than from homes that were rented out year-round.

## Athens: a lot of Airbnb, an even larger stock

In the municipality of Athens the ratio runs the other way. The census found **49,837 homes empty and for rent**, about 3.5 for every home on Airbnb today. The 14,031 Airbnb homes are about 1 for every 10 homes where tenants lived in 2021.

This does not mean Airbnb puts no pressure on rents in particular neighbourhoods. Still, in the [neighbourhood comparison](/en/data/airbnb/#dashboard), asking rents in Athens' neighbourhoods rose at a similar pace almost everywhere over the last three years, and not faster where Airbnb is denser.

## What the official figures say about the platforms

Since 2018 Eurostat has published, by area, the guest nights booked through Airbnb, Booking, Expedia and Tripadvisor. In Greece they reached **52 million in 2025, up from 24 million in 2019**. That was about 25% of all paid nights, counting the platform nights together with those at hotels and other tourist accommodation.

{% include article-chart.html id="platforms-share-of-nights-2025" %}

The share is highest in the cities: 47% in Central Athens and 40% in Thessaloniki. It is lowest on the big-hotel islands: 10% on Rhodes and Kos, 13% in Heraklion. The Cyclades, at 31%, have the most platform nights per resident: about 49 a year for every permanent resident.

## What to keep in mind

- Airbnb is measured today (September 2026), the dwellings in 2021. The comparison shows orders of magnitude, not which homes changed use.
- A home let short-term in October 2021 may have been counted in the census as empty or secondary.
- Eurostat's nights cover all the platforms, not only Airbnb, and are experimental statistics. For 2024 the regional figures do not add up to the national total, so we compare 2025 with 2019.
- For Athens, the Airbnb figures cover the municipality of Athens only, not all of Attica.

The figures by municipality, with the asking rents from our own listings, are on the «Airbnb & ενοίκια» page of the [Airbnb dashboard](/en/data/airbnb/#dashboard).
