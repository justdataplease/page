On Milos, Tinos and Sifnos there are more than three times as many entire homes on Airbnb as the **2021 census** found empty and for rent. On Mykonos and Santorini there are about 3 homes on Airbnb for every 10 dwellings. Athens is the other way round: homes standing empty for rent outnumber the homes on Airbnb 3.5 to 1.

## On the islands, Airbnb is a large share of the homes

Mykonos now has **322 entire homes on Airbnb per 1,000 dwellings**, Santorini 280, Milos 228 and Paros 213. Chania has 89 and Rhodes 79. The big cities are far lower: 32 in Athens, 20 in Thessaloniki and Heraklion.

The municipality of Athens has the most homes on Airbnb in absolute terms (14,031), but relative to the size of the city Airbnb is much thinner there than on the islands.

## More homes on Airbnb than were standing empty for rent

The 2021 census also recorded how many homes were **empty and for rent**. On Milos, Tinos and Sifnos there are now more than 3 homes on Airbnb for each of them, on Naxos 3 and on Paros 2.2. On Mykonos and Santorini the ratio is 1.7 and 1.4.

{% include article-chart.html id="airbnb-census-per-vacant-for-rent" %}

On these islands, few homes were let year-round even before Airbnb. On Naxos only 21% of the occupied homes had a tenant in 2021, and in Apokoronas near Chania 15%, against 42% in Athens. Holiday and secondary homes, on the other hand, were about half of all homes on Paros and Naxos. Many of the Airbnb homes probably come from that stock rather than from homes that were rented out year-round.

## Athens: a lot of Airbnb, an even larger stock

In the municipality of Athens the ratio runs the other way. The census found **49,837 homes empty and for rent**, about 3.5 for every home on Airbnb today. The 14,031 Airbnb homes are about 1 for every 10 homes where tenants lived in 2021.

In Central Athens almost 1 in 2 paid guest nights is booked through platforms such as Airbnb and Booking, according to Eurostat's experimental statistics for 2025. In the Cyclades there are about 49 such nights a year for every permanent resident.

## Rents rose alike, with or without Airbnb

This does not mean Airbnb puts no pressure on rents in particular neighbourhoods. But in the [neighbourhood comparison](/en/data/airbnb/#dashboard), asking rents in Athens' neighbourhoods rose at a similar pace almost everywhere over the last three years. The link between how dense Airbnb is and how much the rent rose is close to zero.

## What to keep in mind

- We count listings, not bookings. Listings that are no longer rented out may stay open and inflate the numbers slightly.
- Airbnb is measured today (September 2026), the dwellings in 2021. The comparison shows orders of magnitude, not which homes changed use.
- A home let short-term in October 2021 may have been counted in the census as empty or secondary.
- For Athens, the Airbnb figures cover the municipality of Athens only, not all of Attica.

The figures by municipality, with the asking rents from our own listings, are on the «Airbnb & ενοίκια» (Airbnb and rents) page of the [Airbnb dashboard](/en/data/airbnb/#dashboard).
