The median price declared in home transfer contracts rose **43%** from 2022 to 2025, faster than the asking prices in listings (27%). In 2022 it was **31% below** the zone price, the official "objective value" of the area; in 2025 it was **2% above**. Listings still ask much more, but the gap narrows every year.

- **2025:** the median asking price was **€2,378 per m²** and the declared contract price **€1,272**.
- **The gap:** listings asked 110% more than the contracts in 2022, 95% in 2023, 92% in 2024, 87% in 2025 and, this year up to September, 83%.

## Declared prices passed the zone price

Every contract in the register of the **Ministry of Finance (AADE)** also carries the zone price of its area. In 2022 the median declared price was €891 per m², while the median zone price of the same homes was €1,300. In 2025 it was €1,272 against €1,250. Asking prices are almost double the zone price (€2,378 in 2025).

The data do not say why. Transaction prices may have genuinely risen, or contracts may now declare an amount closer to what is actually paid. Most likely both are happening.

## Part of the gap is the mix

The asking price is an offer. The declared price is the one written in the contract. And the homes that sell are old: in 2025 their median age was **46 years**, and 6 in 10 (61%) were in buildings 40 years or older, with a declared price of **€1,156** per m². Buildings up to 4 years old were only 6% and sold at **€2,187**.

{% include article-chart.html id="contracts-price-by-building-age" %}

We cannot separate how much of the gap is a difference in quality, how much is negotiation and how much is something else. That is why we do not call it a "discount": we compare two different things, an offer and an agreed price, and the second is mostly old homes.

## Where the gap is largest

In all 13 regions listings ask more than the contracts show. The gap is largest in the **South Aegean** (179%: €3,481 asked, €1,246 declared) and in Crete (144%), and smallest in **Western Macedonia** (62%), **Attica** and Western Greece (65%). In Attica the asking price was €2,692 and the declared price €1,631.

{% include article-chart.html id="asking-vs-contracts-regions" %}

In the big municipalities the gap is below the national average. In Athens the asking price was €2,346 and the declared price €1,506 (56%, from 3,080 contracts), in Thessaloniki €2,561 and €1,534 (67%, from 1,237 contracts). In small municipalities there are few contracts and the comparison does not hold, which is why the dashboard table starts with the municipalities with the most contracts.

## What to keep in mind

- The register has no names or addresses: it has the municipality, the property category, the area, the year of construction, the floor, the zone price and the price.
- Of the **41,742** contracts of 2025 we keep the **17,626** that say something about the price of a home: a dwelling in full ownership, 15 to 500 m², with a price per m² between €100 and €20,000. The rest are plots, parking spaces, storage rooms, shops, shares or bare ownership (without the right of use), which sell at other prices.
- "Median price" means that half the homes are cheaper and half are dearer.

All the figures, by region, municipality and year, are on the «Αγγελίες και συμβόλαια» (Listings and contracts) page of the [property dashboard](/en/data/real-estate/#dashboard).

*Sources: our own market data (sale listings), Ministry of Finance (AADE), register of property transfers. Contracts up to 1 October 2026, listings up to September 2026; 2026 is a partial year. Median prices per m², homes in full ownership. Processing: DataForGreece.*
