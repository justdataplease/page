We put two prices for the same homes side by side: what sellers **ask** in the listings we collect every month, and what was **declared in the transfer contracts**, from the register of the **Ministry of Finance (AADE)**. We compare median prices per m² for homes in full ownership.

- **2025:** the median asking price was **€2,378 per m²** and the declared contract price **€1,272**. Listings asked **87%** more.
- **The gap is closing:** it was 110% in 2022, 95% in 2023, 92% in 2024 and 83% in the part of 2026 that has passed.
- **Who rose faster:** from 2022 to 2025 the declared contract price rose **43%** and the asking price 27%.

## What each price is

The asking price is an offer. The declared price is the one written in the contract for the right that was sold. The register has no names or addresses: it has the municipality, the property category, the area, the year of construction, the floor, the zone price and the price.

Of the **41,742** contracts of 2025 we keep the **17,626** that say something about the price of a home: a dwelling in full ownership of the whole building, 15 to 500 m², with a price per m² between €100 and €20,000. The rest are plots, parking spaces, storage, shops, shares or bare ownership, which sell at other prices.

## Part of the gap is the mix

The homes that sell are old. In 2025 their median age was **46 years**, and 6 in 10 of them (61%) were buildings 40 years or older, with a declared price of **€1,156** per m². Buildings up to 4 years old were only 6% and sold at **€2,187**.

{% include article-chart.html id="contracts-price-by-building-age" %}

We cannot separate how much of the gap is a difference in quality, how much is negotiation and how much is something else. That is why we do not call it a «discount»: we compare two different things, an offer and an agreed price, and the second is mostly old homes.

## Where the gap is largest

In all 13 regions listings ask more than the contracts show. The gap is largest in the **South Aegean** (179%: €3,481 asked, €1,246 declared) and in Crete (144%), and smallest in **Western Macedonia** (62%), **Attica** and Western Greece (65%). In Attica the asking price was €2,692 and the declared price €1,631.

{% include article-chart.html id="asking-vs-contracts-regions" %}

In the big municipalities the gap is lower than the national average. In Athens the asking price was €2,346 and the declared price €1,506 (56%, from 3,080 contracts), in Thessaloniki €2,561 and €1,534 (67%, from 1,237 contracts). On the small islands the gap is much larger but comes from few contracts: 17 in Thira (540%) and 15 in Tinos (370%). That is why the dashboard table starts with the municipalities with the most contracts.

## And the zone price

Every contract in the register also carries its zone price, the objective value of the area. In 2022 the median declared price was **31% below** the median zone price of the same homes (€891 against €1,300). In 2025 it was **2% above** (€1,272 against €1,250). Declared prices passed the objective value, while asking prices are almost double it (€2,378 against €1,250 in 2025).

For scale, for the homes that seem to have been adjudicated at auction the median starting price was €1,203 per m², for another period and another sample; the figures are in the article on auctions.

All the figures, by region, municipality and year, are on the «Αγγελίες και συμβόλαια» page of the property dashboard.

*Sources: our own market data (listings for sale), Ministry of Finance (AADE), register of property transfers. Contracts up to 1 October 2026, listings up to September 2026; 2026 is a partial year. Median prices per m², homes in full ownership.*
