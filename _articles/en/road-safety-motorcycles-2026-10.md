In 2024 **664** people were killed on Greece's roads, **287** of them (43%) on motorcycles and mopeds. Since 2019 more people have been killed each year on two wheels than in cars, with 2022 the only exception. Greece also has the most motorcycles per inhabitant in the EU: **178 per 1,000**, almost five times the median EU country (39).

## Deaths have halved, but far less on two wheels

Since 2010 road deaths have almost halved, from 1,258 to 664 in 2024. But the fall was much steeper in cars (from 545 to 236) than on motorcycles and mopeds (from 403 to 287). From the low of 2022 to 2024, deaths on two wheels rose by 36%, while deaths in cars fell.

## Many motorcycles, but that is not all

No EU country has as many motorcycles per inhabitant. Italy, second, has 131 per 1,000 inhabitants.

{% include article-chart.html id="road-motorcycles-per-1000-2024" %}

Per inhabitant, Greece has the 3rd most road deaths in the EU (64 per million, against 43 in the median EU country). Counted per vehicle, cars and motorcycles, the picture improves but Greece stays high: 0.84 deaths per 10,000 vehicles, 7th of 25 countries.

Young men are most at risk. Men aged 18 to 24 were killed in 2024 at a rate of 202 per million, more than three times the national average.

## When and where

Summer is the worst season: since 2010, an average of 86 people have been killed in August, almost twice as many as in February (47). Per inhabitant, the islands have the most deaths: in 2023 the Ionian Islands had 120 deaths per million inhabitants and the South Aegean 119, almost twice the national rate (62). Attica had 51. Almost half of the 2024 deaths (316 of 664) were on roads inside towns, and only 47 on motorways.

The latest figures show an improvement: in the 12 months to July 2026, **490** people were killed, against 602 in the 12 months before (−18.6%), and serious injuries fell to 451 (−14.7%). These figures are still provisional, though.

## What to keep in mind

- ELSTAT's monthly figures for the latest months are provisional and get revised.
- Motorcycles are the registered ones, not those actually on the road. Motorcycles per inhabitant are our own calculation from Eurostat's figures for the same year; France and Portugal publish no figures for motorcycles in use.
- Deaths per inhabitant are divided by the permanent residents. On the tourist islands, summer visitors use the same roads but are not in the denominator.

All the figures, by month, region, vehicle and country, are on the [road safety dashboard](/en/data/road-safety/#dashboard).

*Sources: ELSTAT (road traffic accidents), Eurostat (road safety, vehicles in use). Processing: DataForGreece.*
