We set **ELSTAT**'s monthly road-accident figures next to **Eurostat**'s data on road deaths, cars and motorcycles in every EU country.

- **Last twelve months:** in the 12 months to July 2026, **490** people were killed on the roads, against 602 in the 12 months before (−18.6%). Serious injuries fell to 451 (−14.7%). These figures are still provisional.
- **Motorcycles:** Greece has **178 motorcycles per 1,000 inhabitants**, the most in the EU and almost five times the median member state (39). In 2024 motorcycles and mopeds accounted for **287 of the 664** road deaths (43%).
- **Cars:** with 597 cars per 1,000 inhabitants in 2025, Greece is slightly above the EU (584).

## Deaths on two wheels are rising again

Since 2010 road deaths have almost halved, from 1,258 to 664 in 2024. But the fall was much steeper in cars (from 545 to 236) than on motorcycles and mopeds (from 403 to 287). Since 2019 more people have died on two wheels than in cars every year except 2022. And from 2022 to 2024 deaths on two wheels rose by 36%, while deaths in cars fell.

## Many motorcycles, but that is not all

No EU country has as many motorcycles per inhabitant. Italy, second, has 131 per 1,000 inhabitants.

{% include article-chart.html id="road-motorcycles-per-1000-2024" %}

Per inhabitant, Greece has the 3rd most road deaths in the EU (64 per million; the median member state has 43). Counted per vehicle, cars and motorcycles in use, the picture improves but Greece stays high: 0.84 deaths per 10,000 vehicles, 7th of 25 countries.

Young men are most at risk. Men aged 18 to 24 were killed in 2024 at a rate of 202 per million, more than three times the national average.

## When and where

Summer is the worst season. Since 2010, an average of 86 people have been killed in August, almost twice as many as in February (47).

{% include article-chart.html id="road-deaths-month-of-year" %}

Per inhabitant, the islands have the most deaths: in 2023 the Ionian Islands had 120 deaths per million inhabitants and the South Aegean 119, almost twice the national rate (62). Attica had 51. Almost half of the 2024 deaths (316 of 664) were on roads inside towns, and only 47 on motorways.

## What to keep in mind

- ELSTAT's monthly figures for the latest months are provisional and get revised.
- Motorcycles per inhabitant are our calculation from Eurostat's figures for the same year. France and Portugal publish no figures for motorcycles in use.
- Deaths per inhabitant are divided by the permanent residents. On the tourist islands, summer visitors use the same roads but are not in the denominator.

All the figures, by month, region, vehicle and country, are on the [road safety dashboard](/en/data/road-safety/#dashboard).
