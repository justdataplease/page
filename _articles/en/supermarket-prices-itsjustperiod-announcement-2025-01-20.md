In January 2025 period products such as pads and tampons were taxed in Greece at the top VAT rate, 24%, the same as cosmetics and cigarettes. The **It's Just Period** group is calling for the tax to be cut or abolished.

Together with the group, we track the prices of these products in supermarkets. If the tax is cut, we will see whether the cut reached the shelf or the chains kept it, as has happened in other European countries.

The prices of every period product, by chain and month, are on the «Προϊόντα Περιόδου» (Period products) page of the [supermarket prices dashboard](/en/data/supermarket-prices/#dashboard).
