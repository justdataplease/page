In 2024 **67,103** new businesses registered with **GEMI**, the General Commercial Registry: about 3 in 4 of those counted by the official statistics in the same sectors (**88,028**). In accommodation, food service and trade the registry sees almost all of them. In health it sees only 1 in 3, because self-employed professionals do not go through GEMI. For 2025, which the official statistics do not cover yet, GEMI already shows **74,031** new businesses, 10% more.

The official figures are the **enterprise births** published by **Eurostat**, which come from ELSTAT's business register. They count every business that started an activity, self-employed professionals included. The gap from GEMI shows where the registry sees well and where it does not.

## Trade and tourism in, self-employed professionals out

In accommodation and food service GEMI recorded **99%** of the new businesses of 2024, and in trade **98%**. In health it recorded only **34%**, in education 46% and in professional and scientific activities 49%. Doctors, lawyers, engineers and accountants register with the tax office without going through GEMI. Coverage is also low in transport (59%).

In three sectors GEMI has **more** new companies than the official count, about 1.3 times as many: energy, finance and real estate. These are most likely companies that do not start a normal activity straight away, such as holding companies: the official statistics count a business only once it has turnover or staff.

## GEMI is a year ahead

Since 2021 new registrations in GEMI have risen every year. The official figures jump more, with **90,224** new businesses in 2022 and **78,754** in 2023, and they stop at 2024. By region, GEMI's coverage in 2023 ranged from **92%** in the South Aegean to **57%** in Western Macedonia.

## What to keep in mind

- GEMI counts registrations by year of incorporation. Eurostat counts businesses that started an activity in the year. The ratio shows coverage, not exactly which businesses are missing.
- We compare the same sectors: industry, construction and market services, without agriculture, public administration and membership organisations. We count head offices only, not branches.
- The official figures are revised, and those for 2024 are still provisional.

All the figures, by sector and region, are on the «ΓΕΜΗ και επίσημα στοιχεία» (GEMI and official data) page of the [businesses dashboard](/en/data/businesses/#dashboard).
