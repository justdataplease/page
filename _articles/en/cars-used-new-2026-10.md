The median used car in our listings in September asked **€9,500**, was **11 years old** and had done **132,500 km**. Almost 6 in 10 ran on petrol. New cars are a different picture: half are hybrids, and fully electric cars are just **6.2%**.

## Among used cars, petrol rules

Of the used cars listed in September 2026, 57% ran on petrol and 28% on diesel. Hybrids were 5%, plug-ins almost 3% and electric cars just over 1%. These are mostly older cars: the median petrol car listed is 16 years old.

The price follows the fuel and the age. The median used hybrid asked €20,500 and was 4 years old.

The median asking price was €1,000 lower than in September 2025, with drops for petrol, diesel and hybrids. But the listings we see change from month to month, so the change shows a trend, not an exact price change.

## Among new cars, hybrids

In 2025 **144,036** new passenger cars were registered. Hybrids (petrol and diesel) were **51%**, against 33% in the EU, and diesel cars have almost vanished (3.4%). Fully electric cars lag behind: 6.2% against 17.3% in the EU, which puts Greece 21st in the EU.

## What to keep in mind

- Listing prices are asking prices, not sale prices. We keep only undamaged used cars between €500 and €200,000.
- "Median" means that half the cars ask less and half ask more.
- Eurostat's hybrids are the ones that do not charge from the socket; plug-ins are counted separately.

All the figures are on the [cars dashboard](/en/data/cars/#dashboard).

*Sources: our own used-car listings, Eurostat (road transport). Processing: DataForGreece.*
