We set road deaths next to the crimes the police record, with data from **Eurostat** and **ELSTAT**, and compared Greece with the other member states. The picture is split: on the roads Greece is still among the worst countries in the EU, while for most crimes it sits around the middle or below.

- **Roads:** **664** people were killed in 2024, **64 per million inhabitants**. That is the 3rd highest rate in the EU, after Romania and Bulgaria. The median member state is at 43.
- **More recent:** in the 12 months to July 2026 ELSTAT counts **490** deaths, against 602 in the 12 months before. These figures are still provisional.
- **Crime:** thefts of vehicles or their parts are **4 times** the rate of the median member state. Robbery, homicide, theft and home burglary are at the middle or lower.

## Fewer deaths, but the gap to the EU stays

In 2000 there were 2,037 road deaths. By 2024 there were three times fewer. But deaths fell all over Europe, at a similar pace: in 2000 Greece had about 1.4 times the deaths per inhabitant of the median member state, and in 2024 about 1.5 times. Since 2020 the Greek rate has crept up, from 55 to 64 per million, while the median stayed at 43.

Most of the dead are drivers: 491 in 2024. Pedestrian deaths were 82, down from 375 in 2000. On average August is the worst month of the year.

## Crime: around the middle, with one exception

For the crimes the police record Greece does not stand out, with one exception: **thefts of vehicles or their parts**, 174 per 100,000 inhabitants in 2024, against 41 in the median member state. That is the 5th highest rate of 25 countries with data.

{% include article-chart.html id="safety-crime-vs-median-2024" %}

Home burglaries fell from 209 per 100,000 in 2014 to 58 in 2024. Robberies concentrate in Attica: 60 per 100,000 inhabitants in 2024, while no other region goes above 21.

## What to keep in mind

- Crime means what the police recorded. Unreported crime is missing, and countries record by different rules, so the comparisons show orders of magnitude.
- We compare with the median member state, not the EU total, because Eurostat's EU-27 road deaths per million do not match its own EU-27 count of deaths.
- ELSTAT's monthly figures are provisional for the last 12 months and get revised.

All the figures, by region, regional unit and country, are on the [safety dashboard](/en/data/safety/#dashboard).
