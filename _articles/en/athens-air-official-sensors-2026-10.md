We set the official air-quality stations in Attica, as the **European Environment Agency** publishes them, next to the sensors of the **City of Athens** "Smart Neighbourhood" programme, which we collect every day.

- **Official stations:** in 2025 the **Patision** station measured an average of **62.0 µg/m³** of nitrogen dioxide (NO2), more than one and a half times the EU limit (40). Piraeus-1 (57.3) and Aristotelous (42.8) were also over the limit: **3 of the 12** stations with complete data.
- **Sensors:** of the City's 97 sensors with enough readings in 2026, **19** average more than 40 for NO2. The highest are in the centre: **Kotzia Square 61.6**, Korai and Klafthmonos 56.1, Omonia 54.6.
- **WHO:** the World Health Organization's guideline for NO2 is 10. It is exceeded by 11 of the 12 official stations and by all 97 sensors.

## The official stations: few, with Patision on top

The national network has 14 stations in Attica and only four inside the Municipality of Athens. In 2025 two of them, Athinas and Votanikos, did not have enough valid hours for an annual mean. Patision is the most polluted station every year: 69.0 µg/m³ in 2022 and 62.0 in 2025. Since 2022, the first year without pandemic lockdowns, NO2 has fallen at 8 of the 10 stations with data for both years; it rose at Piraeus-1 and Liosia.

## What the sensors add

The City's sensors are many and close together: almost a hundred in squares, parks and sports grounds. They show that the problem is not limited to the street of the official station. The three highest are in the 1st Municipal District, around Omonia, followed by squares in Agios Panteleimonas, Kypseli and Petralona.

{% include article-chart.html id="air-sensors-no2-2026" %}

The sensor in Pavlou Bakogianni Square is 190 metres from the Patision station and reads 45.7, less than the station. The official station stands on the avenue, while the sensor is in the square and has been measuring since March, without a winter.

## What to keep in mind

- The City's sensors are low-cost: they show differences between areas and seasons, not readings equivalent to the official network's.
- The sensor averages cover the days with readings up to 21 October 2026, and each sensor started on a different day. They are not annual means, and the EU limit applies to the annual mean.
- The official data are verified up to 2025. Greece does not send up-to-date data to the European Environment Agency, so the two series do not overlap in time.

All the figures, by station, hour and day, are on the [Athens neighbourhoods dashboard](/en/data/athens-neighbourhoods/#dashboard), page «Επίσημοι σταθμοί» (Official stations).
