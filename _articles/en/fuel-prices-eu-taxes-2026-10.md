On 19 October 2026 unleaded 95 cost **€2.191** a litre in Greece, the 5th highest price among the 27 EU countries, where the average was €1.991. Without the taxes, though, it would cost **€1.052**, less than the EU average (€1.097). VAT, excise duty and charges make up **52%** of the price, against 45% in the EU.

- **Cyclades:** had they been a country, they would have the 3rd dearest unleaded in the EU.
- **Diesel:** for diesel Greece sits in the middle: €2.126, 12th of 27, slightly below the EU average (€2.139).

## The difference is the tax

Before taxes, unleaded in Greece ranks 13th of the 27 countries, right in the middle. Taxes lift it to 5th. Spain is the opposite case: its price before taxes is the 2nd highest in the EU, but with low taxes its unleaded falls to 20th.

This is nothing new. Since 2012 unleaded in Greece has been dearer than the EU average every year, with the widest gap in 2022: €2.051 a litre on average, against €1.804 in the EU. Diesel, by contrast, was cheaper than the EU average from 2013 to 2016, and again in 2025 and 2026.

## On the islands, dearer still

The EU bulletin gives the national average, but the gaps between prefectures are wide. On 30 September 2026, when the national average was €2.207, unleaded cost €2.438 in the Cyclades, €2.330 in the Dodecanese and €2.162 in Preveza, the cheapest. At the Cyclades price, only Denmark (€2.658) and the Netherlands (€2.465) had dearer unleaded that week.

The price in every prefecture, week by week, is on the «Νομοί» (Prefectures) page of the [fuel prices dashboard](/en/data/fuel-prices/#dashboard).

## What to keep in mind

- The bulletin's EU average is weighted by each country's consumption. For the early years it covers the countries that were members then, and for the latest weeks it is provisional.
- The EU bulletin has one price per Monday. The prefecture bulletins are daily: the latest we have are from 30 September 2026, which we compared with the EU bulletin of Monday 28 September.
- Prices are not adjusted for each country's price and income levels.

All the figures, every week since 2012 and for every country, are on the «Η Ελλάδα στην ΕΕ» (Greece in the EU) page of the [dashboard](/en/data/fuel-prices/#dashboard).

*Sources: European Commission (Weekly Oil Bulletin), Observatory of Liquid Fuel Prices, Ministry of Development. Processing: DataForGreece.*
