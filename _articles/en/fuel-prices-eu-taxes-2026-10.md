We set the **European Commission**'s Weekly Oil Bulletin, with the prices in every EU country, next to the prices by prefecture from the **Observatory of Liquid Fuel Prices** bulletins we collect.

- **Unleaded:** on 19 October 2026 unleaded 95 cost **€2.191** a litre, the 5th highest of the 27 EU countries. The EU average was €1.991.
- **Taxes:** VAT, excise duty and charges make up **52%** of the price, against 45% in the EU. Before taxes, unleaded in Greece (€1.052) is cheaper than the EU average (€1.097).
- **Diesel:** for diesel Greece sits in the middle: €2.126, 12th of 27, slightly below the EU average (€2.139).

## Above the EU every year

Since 2012 unleaded in Greece has been dearer than the EU average every year. The widest gap was in 2022, when a litre cost €2.051 on average against €1.804 in the EU. Diesel is different: from 2013 to 2016, and again in 2025 and 2026, it was cheaper than the EU average.

## The difference is the tax

Before taxes, unleaded in Greece ranks 13th of the 27 countries, right in the middle. Taxes lift it to 5th. Spain is the opposite case: its price before taxes is the 2nd highest in the EU, but with low taxes its unleaded falls to 20th.

## On the islands, dearer still

The EU bulletin gives the national average. In the bulletins by prefecture the gaps between prefectures are wide. On 30 September 2026, when the national average was €2.207, unleaded cost €2.438 in the Cyclades and €2.330 in the Dodecanese, and €2.162 in Preveza, the cheapest.

Had the Cyclades been a country, that week they would have been the 3rd dearest in the EU, after Denmark (€2.658) and the Netherlands (€2.465).

## What to keep in mind

- The bulletin's EU average is weighted by each country's consumption. For the early years it covers the countries that were members then, and for the latest weeks it is provisional.
- The EU bulletin has one price per Monday. The prefecture bulletins are daily: the latest we have are from 30 September 2026, which we compared with the EU bulletin of Monday 28 September.
- Prices are not adjusted for each country's price and income levels.

All the figures, every week since 2012 and for every country, are on the [fuel prices dashboard](/en/data/fuel-prices/#dashboard), page "Η Ελλάδα στην ΕΕ" (Greece in the EU).

*Sources: European Commission (Weekly Oil Bulletin), Observatory of Liquid Fuel Prices, Ministry of Development. Processing: DataForGreece.*
